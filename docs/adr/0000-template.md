## Context

What situation, problem, or force makes a decision necessary? State facts, not preferences.

## Decision

The decision, stated in full sentences in the active voice ("We will ...").

## Governance review

The three ARGUS Governance Council reviews (per ADR-0009), in order. Record the outcome and reasoning of each, not just "pass":

1. **Constitutional Review** — Does this violate any article of the Constitution? If yes, the proposal stops. No balancing test, no majority vote.
2. **Domain Review** — Does this preserve the ontology and the analytical ladder (Reality → Evidence → Observation → Interpretation → Hypothesis → Understanding → Human Judgment)? No ADR may collapse these layers.
3. **Architectural Review** — Reversible? Maintainable? Operationally realistic? Understandable? Testable? Architecture serves the domain — not the reverse.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what obligations this places on future work. Include the costs honestly — an ADR that lists only benefits is incomplete.

## Alternatives considered

Each rejected alternative and the concrete reason it was rejected.
